# Ledger License Summary

Effective date: July 29, 2026

This is a plain-English summary of the Ledger license model. It is not a substitute for the full End User License Agreement. If this summary conflicts with the EULA, the EULA controls.

## The Short Version

Ledger is desktop accounting software. You can install it and open ledger files without a paid license. Without an active write license, Ledger operates in read-only mode so people can view their own data without being held hostage.

A valid license unlocks write access.

## Read-Only Use

Read-only use is allowed so users can open, inspect, print, export, or review ledger files when they do not have a paid license, when a trial expires, or when they receive a file from someone else.

Read-only mode does not mean every file is guaranteed to open forever. Users are still responsible for backups, file storage, passwords, operating systems, and record retention.

## Trial Licenses

A trial license unlocks write access for a limited time, usually 30 days unless a different period is granted.

The trial period is tied to activation information for the machine, product, and product version. Installing another trial key is not intended to create endless new trials on the same workstation.

We may extend a trial when appropriate. Trial extensions are discretionary and do not convert the trial into a perpetual license unless we say so in writing.

## Perpetual Licenses

A perpetual license allows the licensed version of Ledger to be used indefinitely, subject to the EULA.

Perpetual does not automatically mean:

- free support forever;
- free major-version upgrades forever;
- free access to future separately licensed modules;
- cloud services;
- custom development;
- data migration;
- bookkeeping, tax, payroll, audit, or legal advice; or
- a guarantee that Ledger is suitable for every organization.

## Activations

Licenses are activated per workstation. A standard license is intended for one person's reasonable use and may allow up to five activations so that person can use Ledger on a primary workstation, laptop, home computer, or similar personal machines.

We are not trying to punish normal people for owning more than one computer.

We are trying to prevent one license from becoming an unauthorized company-wide deployment.

If more activations are needed, contact support. We may increase the activation limit when the request is reasonable. We may review, restrict, or deny activation patterns that look abusive, automated, resold, shared, or inconsistent with the license.

## Product and Version Scope

Licenses may be scoped to a product and product version, such as Ledger Desktop 2026.

That means a license for one product, year, edition, or module may not activate another product, year, edition, or module.

This gives us room to offer future annual versions, paid major upgrades, or separately licensed modules without changing the whole licensing system.

## Updates

Ledger may check for updates manually when the user chooses Help > Check for Updates.

Updates may include bug fixes, security fixes, improvements, and new features. Some updates may be free. Some future versions or modules may require a new license.

Users should back up ledger files before installing updates.

McMurry Software may require affirmative acceptance of updated license terms before installing an update or using a new version. Declining updated terms does not retroactively cancel perpetual rights already granted for the purchased version, but the user may not be entitled to the affected update, service, activation, or new version.

## Local Data and Privacy

Ledger is local-first. Your ledger files stay where you put them.

We do not want your ledger files. We do not provide cloud backup or cloud ledger hosting as part of the normal product.

The licensing server receives activation information, license keys, product/version information, and machine-bound activation request data. It does not receive your accounting transactions, payroll records, bank statements, tax filings, customer lists, vendor lists, or ledger database files during ordinary activation.

## What Users Are Responsible For

Users are responsible for:

- making backups;
- protecting ledger files;
- verifying accounting data;
- reviewing reports;
- filing taxes correctly;
- handling payroll correctly;
- securing computers and networks;
- controlling employee/user access;
- deciding whether Ledger is appropriate for their business; and
- getting professional advice when needed.

Ledger is a tool. It is not an accountant, lawyer, payroll company, auditor, tax preparer, or compliance officer.

## Refunds

An unactivated license may be refunded if the purchaser requests the refund within 14 calendar days after purchase. Except where applicable law requires otherwise, a purchase is final after activation because the resulting perpetual offline certificate cannot reliably be retrieved from an offline computer. Duplicate charges and failures to deliver the license will be corrected.
