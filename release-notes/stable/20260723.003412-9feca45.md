### Added

- Added a Cash Flow Statement built through Custom Summary, with Operating, Investing, and Financing sections, beginning and ending cash balances, monthly and comparative columns, drilldowns, and estimated-transaction support.
- Added a normally hidden Reconciliation Difference row that appears when filters or classifications prevent the statement from reconciling.

### Changed

- Credit card accounts now default to the Financing cash-flow designation; account-level overrides remain available.
- Balance Sheet and Financial Summary equity sections present calculated components directly under Equity without changing Ledger's equity calculation rules.
