### Changed

- Prior activity in ordinary equity accounts now rolls into each fund's retained-earnings balance, eliminating the separate Unallocated Beginning Equity line while keeping current-period contributions and distributions visible.
- Balance Sheets and Statements of Changes in Equity now present accumulated earnings using the retained-earnings account's actual number and name.
- Statements of Changes in Equity now separate direct Capital Activity from accumulated Operating Results, with current capital changes broken down by account across total, monthly, and comparison columns.

### Added

- Online Banking can now connect directly to a user-owned SimpleFIN Bridge subscription. Ledger securely stores the connection on the local Windows account, discovers and maps bank and credit-card accounts, downloads pending and posted activity, and applies the existing Core-owned reconciliation workflow without placing banking credentials in the ledger file.
- Connected SimpleFIN feeds refresh automatically when their local data is more than four hours old, while Sync Now remains available for an immediate check. Account setup requires a reconciliation checkpoint within the preceding 90 days.
- Account Ledger Quick Entry now supports split transactions with a register-aligned detail grid, per-line accounts and memos, and a live balance indicator before saving.
- Custom Summary reports now offer print setup for portrait or landscape orientation, actual size, fit-to-columns, fit-to-rows, or fit-to-one-page scaling, with an option to prefer page breaks between major statement groups.
- A historical Cash Flow Statement now classifies activity as operating, investing, or financing and reconciles the net change to beginning and ending cash and cash-equivalent balances.
- A first Statement of Changes in Equity now restores Ledger's former account-based equity rollforward as a separate report, showing beginning equity, retained earnings, current equity-account activity, current-period net income, and ending equity with prior-period comparisons.

### Changed

- Balance Sheet and Financial Summary equity sections now present their calculated components directly under Equity instead of wrapping them in Beginning Equity and Current Period Equity Activity groups. Ledger's equity calculation rules are unchanged, and amount underlines now follow the final displayed statement structure.
- Account Ledger name links now open the transaction represented by that row, matching the other linked ledger cells.

### Fixed

- SimpleFIN checking, savings, and credit-card balances now use Ledger-facing cleared and pending signs consistently, including institutions whose depository balance already includes pending activity.
- Zero-dollar authorization markers remain available as feed evidence without becoming accounting transactions, Home uses the normalized pending total when evaluating feed status, and superseded local feed diagnostics are removed after successful synchronization.
- Vendor Center no longer builds a full trial balance just to obtain its A/P tie-out balance, reducing unnecessary work when the dashboard opens.
